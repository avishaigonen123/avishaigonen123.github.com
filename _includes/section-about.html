About

I'm a security researcher focused on vulnerability research and disclosure - finding exploitable bugs in real products, getting them fixed, and writing up how they worked. Disclosed through GitHub Security Advisories.

I came to this through competition: several hundred CTF challenges across boot2root machines, web exploitation, binary exploitation, crypto and reverse engineering. That habit of documenting every solution is why the writeup archive is as large as it is.

I also like pulling things apart at a lower level - Android application security, mobile instrumentation with Frida, and side-channel work on embedded hardware.
