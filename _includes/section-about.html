I'm a security researcher who spends most of my time on the web: application and API security, authentication logic, and the request-handling code that sits in front of everything else. Lately that has meant the servers AI agents talk to, where the same old assumptions keep showing up in new places.

What I enjoy is the moment you read how something was built and spot the assumption that does not hold. Most of my published work started there, and the CVEs on this site are what happened when the assumption turned out to matter.

I got here through competition rather than a classroom. Several hundred CTF challenges across web exploitation, boot2root machines and crypto taught me more than any course did, and writing every solution down is why the writeup archive is as large as it is.
