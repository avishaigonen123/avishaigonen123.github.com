The cases

  • JBL / HARMAN - open redirect in a recipe link; their fix fell to the @ trick. ($350, CWE-601)
  • Haaretz - open redirect that ships phishing from their own mail (passes SPF / DKIM / DMARC).
  • Haaretz, round 2 - a returnTo open redirect in the login.
  • Haaretz, round 3 - their fix fell to login.haaretz.co.il//attacker (a protocol-relative path).
